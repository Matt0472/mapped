The new website for **Agenxia**, a wine representation agency based in Milan and active since 2009.
Agenxia promotes fine Italian and international wines to restaurants, bars, wine shops and the wider
*Ho.re.ca.* world, building on decades of experience in the field.

The project is a full redesign of their online presence, built in collaboration with
[SNISNI](https://snisni.it).
