A portfolio website I built for **Lorena Brambilla**, a professional photographer based in
Vanzago, on the outskirts of Milan. She specialises in maternity, newborn, children & family,
and pet photography — a kind of work that lives on trust, patience and atmosphere.

Her philosophy, *"Passione. Amore. Vita."* (Passion. Love. Life.), treats photography as a
universal language that captures the soul of a moment and turns it into a lasting memory. The
site had to convey exactly that: warmth, care, and a genuine attention to detail — the same
qualities her clients highlight when they talk about being put at ease in front of the lens.

The result is a clean, image-first showcase built around her work:

- **Portfolio by genre** — maternity, newborns, children & family, and four-legged friends,
  each in its own gallery.
- **Client testimonials** — real voices that speak to the welcoming atmosphere of her studio.
- **Studio & contact** — location, information, and a clear, friendly path to get in touch.
