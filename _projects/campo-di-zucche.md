A one-page Halloween landing site for **Campo di Zucche**, a seasonal pumpkin-field experience
for families held just outside Milan and run by the nature adventure park **Bosco della Luna**.

The site sets the scene for the event — a night-time autumn story with a haunted maze, themed
creative workshops, pumpkin picking and more — and guides visitors straight to booking their
time slot.

- **Storytelling landing** — a single, immersive page that walks through the experience, the
  workshops, the FAQ and the practical info.
- **Ticketing** — a clear path to book a slot, with pricing and dates front and center.
- **Client-editable content** — the team can update copy, prices and dates on their own.

Built with **Astro** and **Tailwind CSS**, content managed through **Pages CMS**, deployed on
**Cloudflare**, with bookings handled via [**TicketingHub**](https://www.ticketinghub.com/it).

Visit the live site: [campodizucchemilano.it](https://campodizucchemilano.it/)
